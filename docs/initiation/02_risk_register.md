# Risk Register

*Corresponds to item G in the original request, moved earlier in the build sequence deliberately — see `../docs/initiation` ordering rationale in the chat response / one-pager. Risks 1, 2, 5 are [CONFIRMED] already flagged (in different words) in `../02_scope_and_assumptions.md`; the rest are [INFERRED] from repo state and data inspection, not yet stakeholder-validated.*

| ID | Risk description | Cause | Impact | Likelihood | Severity | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Regional (PHN) differences in the treatment gap may be misread as real signal when some are within statistical uncertainty of modelled/small-area estimates | NSMHW PHN tables are modelled small-area estimates, not direct regional surveys | Misdirected funding/priority recommendations to a PHN that isn't actually the worst-affected | Medium | High | Report estimates with appropriate caveats on modelled uncertainty; avoid ranking PHNs on point estimates alone where confidence intervals overlap | PO/BA | Open |
| R-02 | Small-cell suppression in source tables blocks the fine-grained regional/demographic breakdowns the project wants | ABS/AIHW privacy protections suppress or wide-band small counts, especially in remote areas | Priority-population analysis (BR-03) may be undeliverable at the intended granularity for some PHNs | High | Medium | Confirm suppression rules during Phase 2 data ingestion before committing to a specific breakdown level; fall back to coarser groupings where needed | Data/BA | Open |
| R-03 | Suicidality and self-harm indicators in the PHN dataset are handled without appropriate care in reporting | Dataset includes sensitive content; no current governance rule for how it's presented | Reputational/ethical harm; risk of sensationalised or clinically inappropriate framing in a public-facing portfolio artifact | Low | High | Aggregate-only reporting, neutral clinical language, explicit editorial review step before any public output touching these tables (see `08_governance_communication_plan.md`) | PO/BA | Open |
| R-04 | Evidence base is already 2–4 years old by the time any output is read | Survey wave 2020–2022, published 2024, analysed 2026, with further lag to any real decision use | Recommendations may not reflect current post-pandemic service conditions | Medium | Medium | State the data vintage prominently in every output; frame findings as directional/baseline rather than current-state fact | PO/BA | Open |
| R-05 | "Treatment gap = need minus service use" is treated as a clean proxy for unmet need | Some people who don't consult a professional do so by choice, not because of a barrier | Overstated case for intervention if gap is partly voluntary non-use | Medium | Medium | Explicitly caveat the proxy's limits in every report; supplement with qualitative/contextual evidence on barriers where available | PO/BA | Open — already partially mitigated via existing assumption note in `../02_scope_and_assumptions.md` |
| R-06 | Discovery/documentation continues to expand while Phase 2 (actual data ingestion/analysis) never starts | Documentation is easier and lower-risk than analysis; strong existing docs create a temptation to keep polishing them | Project stalls indefinitely at "well-documented but empty" — notebooks/, data_processed/, reports/ remain placeholders | Medium | High | Charter and governance plan should hard-gate: no further initiation documents approved until at least one notebook produces `data_processed/wa_treatment_gap_summary.csv` | PO/BA | Open — **highest-priority risk to actively manage** |
| R-07 | No named sponsor or governance authority exists | Project is currently a self-directed case study, not a commissioned engagement | Charter and Business Case approvals in this pack are structurally "self-approved," reducing their credibility as governance artifacts | Low (accepted, given project's actual nature) | Low | Be explicit in every document that sponsor/approval sections are illustrative/role-played unless the project is deliberately run as a simulated stakeholder engagement | PO/BA | Accepted |
| R-08 | Findings could be read as officially endorsed by WA Health, WAPHA, or named NGOs | Documents reference these organisations by name as intended stakeholders | Misrepresentation risk if published without a clear disclaimer | Low | Medium | Add an explicit "independent analytical case study, not an official publication of any named organisation" disclaimer to any public-facing report | PO/BA | Open |
| R-09 | Analyst's available delivery hours are limited and shared with other commitments outside this project | Solo, part-time delivery with no dedicated team | Slower progress than a fully-resourced timeline; risk of stalling before reaching a demoable state | Medium | Medium | Sequence deliberately — treat the sprint plan's weekly-hours assumption (`09_delivery_plan_sprints.md`) as the hard constraint on scope, not the other way around | PO/BA | Open |
