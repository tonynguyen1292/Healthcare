**Phase 1 (data ingestion) is complete.** As of 19/07/2026, the pipeline has ingested and validated all 34 source files from the National Study of Mental Health and Wellbeing — 73,132 rows across national, state/territory, and Primary Health Network levels, verified against manual spot-checks with zero data-quality issues found. This is Charter Milestone M1, delivered ahead of its original target.

**Phase 2 (treatment-gap computation) starts 27/07/2026, as committed.** This date is firm regardless of the capacity note below — it does not depend on how much gets finished afterward, only on the work actually beginning on schedule.

**A capacity change, stated plainly rather than left implicit**: this project is now secondary to another commitment (WA_Mining), which reduces available delivery time from roughly 7 hours/week to roughly 4. Rather than quietly slip dates or silently under-deliver against the original plan, the full delivery schedule has been revised and republished:

- Original estimate: 5 sprints, project complete 20/09/2026.
- Revised estimate: 8 sprints, project complete 01/11/2026.
- **Total scope and hours are unchanged** (74 hours with contingency) — the extension reflects reduced weekly capacity, not new work or discovered problems.

Full revised sprint-by-sprint breakdown: `09_delivery_plan_sprints.md`. Updated milestone targets: `05_project_charter.md`.

**What this means for each stakeholder group**, per the Stakeholder Register's engagement approach:
- State health planners / WAPHA: the WA-vs-national benchmark and PHN comparison views (originally targeted for early September) now land closer to late September — the underlying analysis is unaffected, only the delivery date.
- Community NGOs / GPs: no change to what you'll eventually receive (executive summary at publication); only the publication date moves.

Questions or concerns about this timeline should be raised before Phase 2 begins (27/07/2026) — per the Change Control approach in `08_governance_communication_plan.md`, the sprint-by-sprint plan itself won't be revisited again mid-flight without good reason once Phase 2 is underway.
